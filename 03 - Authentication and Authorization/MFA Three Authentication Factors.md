
## 1. Something You Know 🧠

Information only you should know.

Examples:

- Password
- PIN
- Security Question

---

## 2. Something You Have 📱

A physical device you possess.

Examples:

- Mobile Phone
- OTP
- Authenticator App
- Security Key (YubiKey)
- Smart Card

---

## 3. Something You Are 👤

Your biometric characteristics.

Examples:

- Fingerprint
- Face ID
- Iris Scan
- Voice Recognition
